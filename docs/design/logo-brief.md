# Tin Tin English — Logo brief (for Claude Design)

## Prompt (copy into Claude Design)

Design a logo and app icon for **"Tin Tin English"**, a camera-based motion game that teaches English to Vietnamese primary-school children (grades 1-5). Kids jump, raise hands and move in front of a phone/TV to answer. Visual style: **Space Cadets** (friendly space, rounded shapes, thick soft outlines, bright but not neon).

**Mascot:** Tin Tin, a small round astronaut buddy (white helmet with a big glass visor, two big friendly eyes, tiny antenna with a star tip). Must read at 48 px.

Deliver **3 directions**, each with: (1) full logo (mascot + wordmark), (2) wordmark only, (3) square app icon 1024x1024 safe-zone friendly (works as Android adaptive icon, mascot centred in the inner 66%), (4) one-colour version, (5) light and dark background previews, (6) 48 px / 96 px legibility check.

1. **Jump Star** - Tin Tin mid-jump with arms up, a star trail beneath; motion = the game's core. Wordmark "Tin Tin" big, "English" smaller on a ribbon.
2. **Speech Planet** - the helmet visor is a speech bubble containing "A" (letters + speaking); small ringed planet behind.
3. **Letter Rocket** - a rocket whose body is the letter "T"; Tin Tin peeks from the window.

**Palette:** deep space navy #1B1F4B, cosmic purple #6C4DFF, sky cyan #35D0FF, star yellow #FFD23F, candy coral #FF6B6B, white #FFFFFF. Keep the outline colour consistent.
**Wordmark:** chunky rounded sans, children-friendly, legible for early readers; "Tin Tin" can have the two "i" dots as stars. Latin letters only (Vietnamese diacritics are not needed in the logo).
**Avoid:** copying any existing character, brand or mascot; scary or realistic space imagery; thin lines; text smaller than 10% of icon height; gradients that break in one colour.
**Use:** Android launcher icon, splash screen, store listing, parent gate header, TV mirror (must read from 3 m).
**Export:** SVG + PNG; give the final hex values and the font used so the Flutter theme can match.
